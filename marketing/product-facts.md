# Little Tales: product facts (ground truth for all marketing)

Only claims on this page may be stated as fact in marketing. Anything else needs a cited source or founder confirmation. Last checked against the code: 29 Oct 2026. Update this file when the product changes.

## What it is
- A private scrapbook web app (works in the phone browser and can be added to the Home Screen). It is **not** a native iOS or Android app.
- Live at https://littletales.me. Run by one founder, Anushka Mittal, from India.
- For people aged **18 and over**.

## How it works (VERIFIED in the app)
- Each tale is a photo plus a short thought (**up to 140 characters**) and/or a **voice note (up to 60 seconds)**.
- The photo becomes a **postage stamp** (perforated edge, postmark) on a scrapbook page.
- **Up to 3 tales per day.**
- Decorate with **tape, stickers, paper and doodles**. Sticker group **Festivals** has 10 Diwali stickers (diya, rangoli, sparkler, firework, akash kandil, marigold garland, mithai plate, two greeting labels, row of diyas). Other sticker groups: Vintage and travel, Realistic objects, Editorial, Little joys, Botanical, Food and drink.
- **4 photo filters:** Original, Warm, Soft, Black and white.
- A **daily prompt** (a small question that changes every day; 90 in rotation), shown on Home and when writing a thought.
- Tales live on a **private calendar**. Streak counting exists.
- **Share card:** a picture of a stamp page made for stories (1080 by 1920).
- **"Your week" video:** made on the phone from your tales, with a **choice of 5 original happy tunes** (or no music). Shown on Day 7 of the free week and every Sunday for members.
- **Sign-in:** Google, or a 6-digit code sent to your email. No password.
- **Reminders:** gentle nudges around 1 pm and 8 pm (local time) if you have not made a tale. On iPhone they need the app added to the Home Screen.
- **Voice typing** ("Speak instead of typing") exists but depends on server setup; do not promise it until the founder confirms it is on.
- Subscribers can **download their tales**. Anyone can **delete their account and tales** at any time.

## Free week and lock rules (VERIFIED)
- **7 days free**, everything unlocked, **no card needed** to start.
- On Day 7 the member sees their week video, then the plans.
- If they do not subscribe, the calendar **locks**; tales are **kept for 30 days** (email reminders at 7 days and 1 day left) and then **permanently deleted**. Subscribing within 30 days brings everything back.

## Privacy (VERIFIED against the Privacy page, 23 Oct 2026)
- Private by default. **No public profiles, no feed, no followers.**
- Photos, voice notes and thoughts are **never used for ads**, **never sold**, and **not used to train AI models**.
- Service providers named in the Privacy page: Supabase (storage), Cloudflare (delivery), Dodo Payments (payments), Resend (emails), Google (only if used for sign-in), Microsoft Clarity (usage analytics, with content masked), Sentry (error reports, no content), OpenAI (only if the voice-typing button is used).
