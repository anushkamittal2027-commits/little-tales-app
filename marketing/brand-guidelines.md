# Little Tales: brand guidelines (for all content)

## Who we are
Little Tales is a small, warm, private scrapbook. A person keeps their days as postage stamps. The brand is a friend with a pen, not a company with a megaphone.

## Voice
- **Warm, small, handwritten.** Short sentences. First person singular from the founder ("I built…"), "you" for the reader.
- **Honest.** Say what it does and does not do. No hype, no countdown pressure, no "revolutionary".
- **Specific over grand.** "A photo of your chai and one line" beats "capture life's moments".
- Lowercase is fine in captions for TikTok and Instagram. Reddit and launch posts use normal sentence case.
- Read it aloud: if it sounds like an ad, rewrite it.

## Words to use
little moments, keepsake, scrapbook, stamp, tiny diary, a page for today, ten seconds, cozy, private.

## Words to avoid
productivity, optimise, hack, revolutionary, game-changing, disrupt, users (say "people"), leverage, unlock your potential, AI-powered, "world's first", "best", "#1".

## No AI tells
Do not write "it's not X, it's Y" reveals, "no X, no Y, no Z" lists, or sentences that trail into extra comma clauses. No em dash chains. Vary sentence length.

## Emoji and hashtags
- 0 to 2 emoji per piece; 💌 and 🌼 fit the brand. Never a row of emoji.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 on short video and Instagram, 0 on Reddit and launch sites. Always niche over huge (#digitalscrapbook, #journalwithme, not #love).

## Always true in every piece
- The product name is **Little Tales** (two words, both capitalised). The domain is **littletales.me**.
- Mention **free for 7 days, no card** when there is a call to action.
- Mention **private by default** when the post is about privacy or sharing.
- Add "18+" where a channel requires an age statement.
- No price unless `marketing/product-facts.md` says pricing is final.

## Never
- Fake testimonials, ratings, user counts or press.
- Names or photos of real people without their permission. Member stamps are used only with consent.
- Guilt or fear ("you will lose your memories"). Use warmth instead.
- Copyrighted music or images. Use the app's own tunes, original sounds, our own photos, or images with a free licence that allows commercial use (check the page).

## Visual identity
- **Look:** warm paper, soft gingham (sky blue or rose), a real perforated postage stamp, tape, small hand-drawn flowers.
- **Colours:** olive `#5E7A53` (buttons, accents), deep olive `#3F5737`, olive wash `#EEF3EA`, ink `#2E3134`, link blue `#3D6A99`, paper cream `#FBF8F2`, rose ink `#7A3E35`.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display (headlines and words that matter), Inter (interface and body), DM Mono (stamp text and dates), Caveat (handwritten notes).
- **Imagery:** bright, everyday and special at once: a coffee machine, a sunflower bouquet, a camper van under a palm tree. Avoid dark, grim or stock-photo-corporate images.
- **Screenshots:** capture the live app at 390 by 844 pixels, device scale 3. Show a finished stamp page, not an empty screen.
- Always add alt text and, for video, on-screen captions.

## Channel notes
- **TikTok, Reels, Shorts:** a hook in the first 2 seconds, show the stamp pressing, captions on screen, under 20 seconds, one idea.
- **Reddit:** value first, honest, no sales language, follow each subreddit's rules, answer every comment, never post the same text in several communities on one day.
- **Pinterest:** keyword-rich titles, vertical 2:3 images, descriptions that read like search phrases.
- **Instagram:** the story card is the main asset; carousels tell a small story.
- **Launch sites:** plain tagline, one clear image, honest first comment from the maker.
- **DMs:** short, personal, one ask, easy to say no to. Say clearly if access is free or gifted (disclosure).
