# Little Tales Terms of Use

**Written in plain words.**

Last updated: 17 October 2026

## The short version
Little Tales (run by Anushka Mittal, Gurgaon, Haryana, India) is a private scrapbook app. You get a **free week**. After that, you subscribe to keep using it. If you don't, your calendar locks, we keep your tales for **30 days**, and then we **delete them for good**.

## Who can use it
You must be 18 or older. You are responsible for what you add and must have the right to add it. Please do not upload anything illegal, or photos or recordings of people who haven't agreed.

## The free week
Your free week starts the day you first sign in and lasts 7 days (a day ends at midnight in your own time zone). No card is needed to start. On day 7 we show you a "Your week" video and the plans.

## Subscriptions
- **Monthly** Rs 149 or **Yearly** Rs 999. Prices are in Indian rupees. If GST applies to us, it is shown clearly at checkout before you pay.
- One plan, everything included.
- Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI AutoPay or card). You can cancel any time from your Account. Your plan renews automatically each month or year until you cancel. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not refund part-used periods, except where the law requires it. If something went wrong with a payment, write to hellolittletales@gmail.com and we will look into it.
- We may change prices. We will tell you before a change affects you.

## If you do not subscribe
After day 7, your calendar and tales are locked. We keep them safe for 30 days and remind you with 7 days and 1 day left. Subscribe in that time and everything comes back as it was. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and we cannot bring them back.

## The 3-tales-a-day limit
You can keep up to 3 tales a day, for free and paid members alike. Deleting a tale on purpose frees its place.

## Your tales are yours
You own your photos, voice notes and words. You give us permission only to store them and show them to you, so the app can work. We do not use them for ads, sell them, or share them.

## Our app
The Little Tales name, look, artwork and software belong to us. Please do not copy them.

## Keeping things running
We try to keep the app working, but we can't promise it will never go wrong. We recommend downloading your scrapbook from time to time. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that we could not reasonably prevent. 

## Ending your account
You can delete your account any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
