# Little Tales Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

**This is a plain-words draft. It is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before launch, especially against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.**

Last updated: [date]

## Who we are
Little Tales is run by [your name / business name], [address]. You can reach us at hellolittletales@gmail.com. Our contact for privacy questions and complaints is [name], at the same address.

## What this app does with your things
Little Tales turns your photos and thoughts into stamps in a private scrapbook. Everything you add is **private to you**. We have no public profiles, no feed and no sharing.

## What we collect
- **Your sign-in details:** your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and Google profile picture address.
- **Your tales:** the photos you take or upload, the voice notes you record, the words you write, the titles, the dates you tag, and the stickers and tape you place.
- **Your plan:** whether you are on the free week or subscribed, and your payment status. **We never see or store your card number or UPI details.** Payments are handled by Razorpay.
- **Reminders:** if you turn them on, a code from your phone that lets us send you notifications, and your time zone.
- **The 4th-tale count:** we count how often someone tries to make a fourth tale in one day, so we can decide if three a day is the right limit. This does not include what is in your tales.
- **Basic technical data** like the type of phone and browser, to keep the app working.

## What we do not do
- We **never use your photos, voice notes or thoughts for ads**.
- We never sell your data.
- We never share your tales with anyone.
- We do not use your tales to train AI models.

## Why we use your data
To run your scrapbook, keep it safe across your phones, remind you when you ask us to, make your "Your week" videos, and manage your free week and subscription.

## Who helps us run the app (our service providers)
- **Supabase**: stores your account, tales, photos and voice notes.
- **Cloudflare**: delivers the app to your phone.
- **Razorpay**: handles payments.
- **Resend**: sends sign-in codes and warning emails.
- **Google**: only if you choose Google sign-in.
They may process data on our behalf. They are not allowed to use it for their own purposes. [Add where the servers are located.]

## How long we keep things
- While you use the app, we keep your tales.
- If your free week ends and you do not subscribe, your calendar locks and we keep your tales for **30 days**. We email you with 7 days and 1 day left. If you subscribe in that time, everything comes back. After 30 days, your tales are **permanently deleted**.
- If you delete your account, we delete your account and all your tales straight away.
- Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

## Your choices and rights
- **Download your scrapbook** (while you are subscribed): Account, Download my scrapbook.
- **Delete your account and tales** at any time, even when locked: Account, Delete my account and tales.
- **Turn reminders on or off** any time.
- You can ask us to see, correct or remove your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hellolittletales@gmail.com. We will reply within [30] days.
- You can complain to us first. If you are still unhappy, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## Who can use Little Tales
Little Tales is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used the app, tell us and we will delete their data.

## Safety
Your tales are stored privately, in folders only your account can open, and the connection is encrypted. No system is perfectly safe. If something goes wrong with your data, we will tell you promptly and tell the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
