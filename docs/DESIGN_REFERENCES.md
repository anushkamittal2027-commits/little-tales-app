## 4. Why the current version looks "kiddish and unreal" (my own diagnosis)

1. **The sample photos are cartoon illustrations.** Real apps show real photographs. Flat vector scenes make the whole page look like a children's book. This is the biggest reason.
2. **The stickers have thick black outlines and flat bright colours.** That reads as a kids' sticker sheet. Grown-up scrapbooks use softer, printed-looking paper stickers.
3. **No real material.** The page is a flat cream rectangle with one soft shadow. Real scrapbooks have paper that has depth, tape that is see-through and slightly shadowed, and stamps with a paper edge.
4. **Bright accents everywhere.** The red button, red postmark and orange mic all shout at the same volume.
5. **Rounded, friendly buttons and chips** add to the toy feel.

## 5. Proposed design direction: "a well-kept photo album at golden hour"

This is my recommendation, not something copied from the apps above.

- **Real photographs only.** Use free-licence photos for samples (Unsplash or Pexels) or your own. No illustrations as photos.
- **Quieter colour.** Keep your five brand colours, but use Postal Red only for the main button and the postmark. Use deeper, warmer neutrals for the page, and a darker "table" behind pages so paper has something to sit on.
- **Paper with depth.** A subtle paper grain, layered shadows (a tight one and a soft wide one), and a slight curl or edge highlight on pages.
- **Realistic stamps.** A real paper edge, a slight emboss, and a postmark that looks inked with a little unevenness. Keep it under 2 seconds.
- **Realistic tape and stickers.** Translucent tape with a faint shadow and torn ends. Stickers that look printed, with a thin white die-cut border and a soft shadow, instead of black outlines.
- **A more refined type scale.** Keep Fraunces, Courier Prime and DM Sans, but use larger, calmer titles, finer weights and more space.
- **Calmer buttons.** Smaller radius, less shadow, and a quiet secondary style.
- **A dark mode, "After dark"**, so the app feels premium at night.
